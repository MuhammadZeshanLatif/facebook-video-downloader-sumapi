How to Use FB Video Downloaders

This step-by-step guide explains how to download public Facebook videos and Reels using a browser. No app installation and no Facebook login required. Works on Android, iPhone, and desktop.

5-Step Download Guide

  1. Copy the Facebook video link — open the public Facebook video or Reel in your browser and copy the URL from the address bar.
  2. Paste the link into the downloader — go to fbvideodownloaders.com and paste the full URL into the input box.
  3. Click the download button — click "Analyze Link" to process the URL and fetch available quality options.
  4. Choose video quality — select HD for best quality or SD for a smaller file size.
  5. Save the video to your device — click Download for your chosen quality. The video saves to your downloads folder.

How to Use on Android

  1. Open Facebook in your Android browser (Chrome recommended)
  2. Find the public video or Reel you want to save
  3. Copy the URL from the browser address bar
  4. Open FB Video Downloaders in a new tab
  5. Paste the link and tap Download — video saves to your Downloads folder

How to Use on iPhone

  1. Open Facebook in Safari or Chrome on your iPhone
  2. Navigate to the public video or Reel
  3. Tap Share, then Copy Link
  4. Open FB Video Downloaders in your browser
  5. Paste the link and tap Download
  6. Use the share icon to save the video to Files or Camera Roll

How to Use on Desktop

  1. Open Facebook in your desktop browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
  2. Click on the public video to open its own page
  3. Copy the full URL from the address bar
  4. Visit fbvideodownloaders.com
  5. Paste the link and click Analyze Link
  6. Choose HD or SD and click Download

Common Issues and Fixes

  • Video not found: The video may be private — only public videos can be downloaded
  • Invalid URL error: Make sure you paste the complete URL including https://
  • Download not starting: Try a different browser or disable ad blockers
  • Low quality only: Some videos are only available in SD — HD is not always possible
  • Reel not working: Open the Reel in a browser instead of the Facebook app
